Enemy Level Scale Download

Sets enemy levels to the average of your top 10 units — mission stickers and wandering squads when you engage them. No editor required.

  • Late story floors (Z1 / Z2 / Sorm / Elheim Z) never scale down
  • Sigil Trials stay at vanilla levels
  • After updating: fully quit Ryujinx and clear the game CPU cache if an old build was installed
  • Unzip, then copy the enemy_level_scale folder into Ryujinx mods and enable it
